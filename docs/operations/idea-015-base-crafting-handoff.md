Cleared storage/closed tabs are not recoverable; tabs do not coordinate. Unknown outcomes require exact replay or separately authorized investigation, not a new UUID to repeat the intended craft. Unknown legacy slot-only/external callers must adopt item_id and completion_id; no speculative fallback with fresh UUIDs.
