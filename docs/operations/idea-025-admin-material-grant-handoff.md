## Authority, transaction and locking

- Existing admin-users authentication derives verified actor and steward/overlord role; new action **award-material** calls exactly one service RPC without a forwarded user JWT. SQL rejects non-null auth.uid, locks/rechecks exactly one actor-role row; absent, unsupported or ambiguous multi-role authority refuses. Existing role management is not changed. No browser-supplied actor/role/cap accepted.
- Lock sequence: actor role SHARE → operation UUID advisory → target character **NO KEY UPDATE** → catalogue SHARE → material row/UPSERT → private completion/audit rows. Character-first serializes supported crafting/sale/inventory writes and ownership/deletion changes. NO KEY UPDATE remains compatible with lifecycle/FK KEY SHARE, avoiding a child material→character lock inversion while waiting for catalogue/material work. No node/encounter locks are acquired after character locking; no Combat2 presence, equipment, resource, world or progression write occurs.
- Active means existing, non-soft-deleted lifecycle target, not necessarily living: a dead non-deleted character may receive materials without healing/revival. New deleted-target grants refuse. Replays recheck current role and Steward target ownership but may confirm an earlier committed grant after deletion or balance consumption, without another material mutation. Role loss/ambiguous role/self prohibition may deny replay; retain UUID for support, never silently remint.
- Missing material row starts at zero. Existing negative balance refuses, never repaired. Additive UPSERT predicates and int4 overflow checks preserve intervening consumption/increment; unexpected returned balance refuses the transaction. Unique key is (character_id,material_key). No legacy add_material route, unchecked raw browser DML, XP, gold or equipment writer is activated.
- UUID binds actor, character, exact material/quantity, normalized reason and policy version via SHA256 digest. Exact replay returns **identical committed JSON**, not a stale original balance. Different payload/actor/target conflicts; a new UUID is a new intentional grant. Material update, completion proof and audit insert share one transaction; any error rolls everything back. No compensation/refund/outbox path.
