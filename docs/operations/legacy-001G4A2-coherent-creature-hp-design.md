# ENG-LEGACY-001G4A2 — Coherent shared creature HP and OOC regeneration

2026-10-15; starting/fetched checkpoint `d73948ed14de8ebe18c1f0e6cbf8093d0a1db09e`. **Design assessment only; RECOMMENDED, NOT APPROVED OR IMPLEMENTED.** G4-R1, E13 and G/ENG-LEGACY-001 remain OPEN. No SQL, schema, runtime, deployment or gameplay change; no hosted/database access.

## Recommendation

Use **option C, a minimal transition toward option A**: retain creatures.hp as the durable per-creature/per-generation record, keep node_creature as the existing Combat2 working projection, and require a single transactional HP-write protocol to synchronize both. During combat, only canonical Combat2 outcomes change HP. Outside combat, one bounded maintenance boundary settles approved elapsed regeneration and updates any matching retained projection before it can be claimed/reactivated.

Keep the current claim/resolver shape initially; do not rewrite all claim reads or introduce a new creature service/template architecture. This is viable only if coherent writes and claim/version fencing are enforced, not by declaring two independent authoritative HP columns. A durable elapsed-time cursor is necessary for restart/retry correctness; existing damage/death/touch timestamps do not supply that contract. “Existing tables and locks” does **not** imply a safe zero-schema change.

This recommendation follows the owner-proposed requirements in this task, not a decision to implement them. In particular, gradual wake recovery replaces the existing unconditional full-heal behavior and needs explicit owner approval. Regeneration rate, delay, accounting precision and catch-up policy remain undecided.

## Proposed transactional boundary

1. Identify exact creature UUID/generation and node binding from authoritative rows; discover required old/new nodes before dependent locks. Acquire existing sorted node advisory coverage, then encounter(s), then dependent rows in the order compatible with current commit/seed. Revalidate after locking; refuse/retry changed coverage without late node locks.
2. Determine canonical combat ownership and unresolved obligations under those locks. Never infer eligibility solely from status='ended', zero players, hp>0 or old-table exclusions. Resolve any current-generation projection, including retained ended rows and a binding at another node.
3. For eligible OOC work, compute server-derived elapsed credit from durable accounting state. If a live claim exists, defer this creature, not steal that claim. For a real maintenance HP mutation, fence any stale/expired proposal via encounter version/token state; serialize with new claims and seed. If hp/cursor/projection consistency cannot be established, fail without an invented reconciliation.
4. Commit HP, matching projection, accounting state and necessary version changes together. Do not modify alive/dead, spawn generation or rewards to perform a heal. Canonical damage continues its existing atomic mirror and additionally participates in eligibility/accounting transitions when that extension is separately approved.
5. Repeated entry, heartbeat phase or retry at the same already-accounted time must not credit again. Cap HP at the authoritative max; do not bank healing while full for later damage. No client-supplied elapsed time, rate, HP or settlement horizon.

These are proposed invariants, not SQL or a completed lock proof. Commit's existing encounter→dependent writes cannot be casually inverted by a maintenance creature-row-first loop. The no-encounter-yet case still requires serialization with seed/entry creation. Multiple creature rows in one encounter share its version: batching must invalidate/fence coherently, not advance tick or fabricate commits/rewards.

### Eligibility requires an explicit owner decision

Suggested conservative first contract for review: suppress regeneration if a living participating fighter can still engage that creature, a live claim exists, or relevant pending action/hostile intent/unconsumed transition/effect can still damage or be damaged by it. Treat uncertainty about relevance as blocked, not as permission to heal. Scope may initially be encounter-conservative for unresolved combat work, to avoid inventing a new target-dependency framework.

A living engaged flag can remain true after all players leave; it must not alone create an undocumented permanent healing ban. Conversely, zero players must not allow healing through an offscreen DoT/cast. A technically active encounter can eventually contain a quiet OOC creature once canonical obligations are resolved under the approved definition. Maintenance need not end/delete the encounter to heal, but this classification is a proposed rule requiring approval, not a fix inferred from F10. Existing encounter-end behavior is not changed by this design assessment.

Separate **write deferral** from **elapsed eligibility**: a live claim prevents maintenance from writing, but does not by itself establish that an otherwise quiet creature fought throughout that interval or erase already earned OOC time. Retain accounting credit while deferred; canonical combat/blocker transitions determine which intervals are eligible. Select the authoritative effective transition time (not a client timestamp), capture a settlement cutoff after locks, and reject a cutoff behind the durable cursor. Exact event/commit-time treatment is part of the next contract decision.

Define which effects/actions are relevant: harmful periodic/retaliatory/AoE work, queued hostile actions, due unresolved casts and applicable entry/exit events need coverage; cosmetic metadata, cooldown records or an unrelated permanent character stance must not automatically block forever. Exact source fields/target scope and fail-closed handling need the next bounded contract step. No approved exhaustive blocker list is asserted here.

## Minimal durable accounting and sleep

Zero added state is not credible for exact elapsed credit: last_damaged_at records damage, died_at records death, and node_creature.updated_at also changes for tank/engagement/actions. None records “already paid through time T while continuously eligible.” Using them as cursors risks double healing or counting combat time as OOC.

**Proposed minimum explicit model:** a per-current-generation accounted-through timestamp and a nullable continuous-OOC-eligible-since timestamp on the durable creature row. Names/types are illustrative, not approved schema. The null eligibility start denotes blocked/dead/unknown state. Existing spawn_seq scopes/reset semantics; no new generation ID or table. A one-cursor alternative is possible only if all eligibility transitions are atomically recorded with equivalent unambiguous state; that coverage has not been demonstrated and is not presumed to save a column.

- On entry into combat/blocking work, settle only previously proved eligible time up to the transition if policy permits, then close eligibility and advance/reset the accounting boundary. On canonical release of the last blocker, record the new eligible start server-side. Damage cooldown, if retained, shifts the earliest eligible time rather than granting combat-time credit.
- All eligibility-changing authoritative paths must participate: combat entry/damage/last-blocker resolution, departure where relevant, effects/actions, quiet reassignment, death/respawn and approved max-HP/config changes. Merely updating these fields when a heal runs is insufficient to reconstruct intervening blocked intervals.
- At full HP advance/close the usable credit window so no stored healing restores later combat damage. Dead rows carry no usable regeneration credit; ordinary respawn restores the existing full-health/new-generation state and initializes fresh accounting. Never heal an old generation into a new one.
- At introduction initialize from an approved cutover observation time, with no invented historical OOC credit. Existing mismatches, if any, require individually approved reconciliation; do not choose the higher, lower, authored or runtime value silently. No data repair is authorized or presumed necessary by this task.

**Sleep accounting proposal:** for a creature proven continuously OOC across sleep, later settlement can account eligible elapsed wall time using bounded arithmetic, not one iteration per missed heartbeat. Rate/quantization and any catch-up horizon/cap are owner decisions; full HP bounds the benefit. No combat ticks, damage, respawn rewards, XP or other simulation work are replayed by regeneration.

If combat effects/actions remain unresolved when sleeping, keep eligibility closed. Do not use their wall-clock expiry alone to invent when combat stopped while its resolver was suspended. Suggested initial policy: let canonical resolution establish release on resume and grant no retroactive OOC credit for that unresolved interval. Whether combat effects should resolve elapsed sleep differently is a separate owner decision, not part of a stealth regeneration fix.

**Scheduling integration:** use the existing authoritative heartbeat's bounded maintenance phase and settle before entry/seed where required under compatible locks. No new cron/timer. Existing read-only roster RPC remains read-only; it can display the last committed HP until the next supported settlement, not mutate invisibly on SELECT. Wake must not unconditionally full-heal or acquire an unbounded set of encounter locks after its trigger/publication DDL. Replace that delegation only in a separately approved cutover; no global wake rewrite is assumed. Bounded batches may defer other creatures to a later opportunity, with cursor credit retained.

Rounding/retries: either retain partial eligible time in an anchored interval cursor or explicitly store the approved remainder; do not floor every tiny elapsed delta and then discard all time. The two-timestamp model is sufficient only for the selected time-quantization contract; a continuous/fractional design may need additional residual state. This must be chosen and tested before schema minimization is claimed. No regeneration period/rate or new balance formula is finalized here.

## Decisions requiring owner approval

1. **Authority:** approve C→A as the target, including immutable creature/generation identity and synchronized retained projections; do not approve two independently writable HP surfaces.
2. **Combat/OOC boundary:** exact relevant blocker scope; handling no-player engaged creatures, unresolved effects/actions and technically active quiet encounters; damage cooldown and when OOC begins.
3. **Timing/balance:** rate basis (fixed HP or percentage, and applicable max), interval/continuous quantization, delay, rounding/remainder and maximum catch-up horizon if any. Today's10%-per-helper-call/5minute guard and full wake heal are historical behavior, not automatically the proposed settings.
4. **Sleep:** eligible OOC wall-time credit and explicit policy for unresolved combat intervals. Approve any necessary limited departure from current sleep semantics through the canonical specification when rules are actually selected; no unbounded catch-up or second heartbeat.
5. **Schema/accounting:** minimal durable cursor/eligibility metadata, initialization/no historical credit and reset semantics; no new table/service by default.
6. **Mutation/cutover boundaries:** legacy healing delegates must stop independent writes; generic Admin full-HP saves, max-HP changes and node reassignment must use/refuse according to the new protocol. Coordinate with the separately pending G4B boundary rather than silently redesigning Admin. Permission changes, parity and conflicting existing data need separate scoped authorization.
